# Worktree cleanup

Inventory absolute paths, branches, bases and active owners. Inspect tracked, staged, untracked and ignored work. Check whether commits have an independent reachable ref and whether any session or process still uses the path. Mark unknown ownership or unique work as a hold.

Present the exact proposed removals, retained work and recovery implications. Keep the inventory read-only until approval covers the destructive payload. Remove only the approved idle targets using their host's normal lifecycle. Re-list survivors and report what was actually reclaimed.
